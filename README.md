# My Favorite Series: *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba*

> "No matter how many people you may lose, you have no choice but to go on living." — Tanjiro Kamado

**Demon Slayer** is a Japanese dark fantasy action anime series following **Tanjiro Kamado**, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered and his younger sister, *Nezuko*, is turned into a demon.

## Why I Recommend It
1. **Stunning Animation:** Ufotable's visual effects and fluid sword-fighting choreography are unmatched.
2. **Emotional Storytelling:** Every character—including the demons—has a compelling backstory.
3. **Memorable Soundtrack:** Intense orchestral scores paired with iconic opening themes.
